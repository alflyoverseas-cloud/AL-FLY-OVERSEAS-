Who We Are

Your Dreams • Our Mission

AL-FLY OVERSEAS is an India-based overseas recruitment and manpower services agency. We aim to connect suitable candidates with international employment opportunities and help employers reach relevant talent.

Our team can assist with recruitment enquiries, candidate coordination and information about available opportunities. Candidates should verify job terms, employer details, salary, visa conditions and written agreements before proceeding.
